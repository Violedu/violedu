# Niching Down: Auditions/Exams vs. Recital

> Third step of the avatar-definition approach (Step 1 = [demand-analysis.md](demand-analysis.md),
> Step 2 = [step-2-market-scoring.md](step-2-market-scoring.md)). Step 2's cross-cut flagged
> two viable sub-niches. This step niches each down the specificity ladder, compares them
> head-to-head, and commits to one. Hormozi: *"Riches in niches… don't make me niche-slap you."*

---

## The decision in one line

**Commit to auditions/exams as the primary niche.** It wins the value equation on *both* sides
(provable outcome ↑, less material to master ↓ time & effort), it makes the offer's timeline
*honest*, and its outcomes are *verifiable* — which fixes the offer's weakest driver (perceived
likelihood). Recital is the better *demographic* but the worse *offer* — keep it as a secondary,
higher-tier expansion, not the spearhead.

**The catch you must accept:** this choice is coupled to offer scope. **Auditions/exams ⇒ a
first-movement offer (~6–8 weeks)** — a pivot from your current full-concerto, 12-week program.
**Recital ⇒ the full concerto** (your current offer). You can't niche to auditions *and* keep
selling the full concerto; they're different products. That trade-off is the heart of this step.

---

## The specificity ladder, applied to each

Dan Kennedy's ladder — keep adding specificity until the prospect thinks *"this is for me."*

**Auditions/exams:**
> violinists → advanced violinists → advanced violinists preparing the **Tchaikovsky first
> movement** → …for a **conservatoire entrance audition, orchestral audition, or graded
> diploma (ARSM/LRSM)** with a **fixed date and an external assessor.**

**Recital:**
> violinists → advanced violinists → advanced adult amateurs / returners → …preparing the
> **full Tchaikovsky concerto** for a **self-set recital or personal milestone.**

Notice the audition ladder lands on **less material** (first movement, often only to ~bar 97)
and an **external, dated, pass/fail event.** The recital ladder lands on **the entire 35-minute
concerto** and a **self-imposed, subjective** goal. That single difference drives most of what follows.

---

## Head-to-head comparison

| Decision dimension | **Auditions / Exams** | **Recital** | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain & urgency** | Hard external deadline; career/place stakes → acute | Emotional "Everest" pull; self-imposed → softer | **Audition** |
| **Purchasing power** | Career stakes; often parent-funded *or* adult diploma candidate | Discretionary adult hobby spend | Tie |
| **Offer deliverability** | **First movement only → 6–8 wks is honest & tight** | **Full concerto → 12 wks, only if they already have all 3 mvts** | **Audition (decisive)** |
| **Provability of outcome** | **Binary & verifiable** (got in / passed / distinction) | Subjective ("felt ready," "performed well") | **Audition (decisive)** |
| **Value-equation fit** | Likelihood ↑ (provable) + time/effort ↓ (less material) | Bigger dream outcome, but heavier denominator | **Audition** |
| **Market size** | Narrower; defined annual flow of applicants/candidates | Larger, growing pool (adult returners, 8.9% CAGR) | **Recital** |
| **Seasonality** | **Peaked** (pre-screen deadlines Dec–early Jan; live Jan–Feb) | Year-round, smooth | **Recital** |
| **Targetability** | Excellent (prep networks, teachers, audition-intent search) | Good but more diffuse | Audition |
| **Competition** | Some (tonebase audition tips; audition-prep teachers) | Less direct ("recital coaching" is unclaimed) | Recital |
| **Repeat revenue / LTV** | Transactional, but next audition/next piece recurs | Relationship potential (piece after piece) | Slight recital |
| **Dream-outcome framing** | External validation (a seat, a place, a pass) | Personal mastery + peer status | Tie |

**Score: Audition wins the dimensions that drive conversion and delivery; Recital wins the
dimensions that drive volume and longevity.** For a business *building* its proof and tightening
a risky timeline, conversion + delivery + provability matter more right now.

---

## Why deliverability is the decisive factor

This connects every prior analysis. Auditions and exams **don't require the full concerto:**

- Conservatoire **pre-screens require "one movement of a concerto"** and a recorded video — the
  Tchaikovsky **first movement** is a textbook pre-screen/audition choice.
- Orchestral and entrance auditions typically **hear only the exposition + cadenza** (to ~bar 97).
- **Diplomas (ARSM 30-min / LRSM 45-min programmes)** are built from a *programme*, not a single
  full concerto — a first-movement Tchaikovsky can anchor it.

So the **same first-movement product serves conservatoire pre-screen, orchestral audition, *and*
diploma** — maximally reusable, and **honestly deliverable in 6–8 weeks** for a player who already
gets through the movement (per the timeline research).

The recital is the *one* use case that demands the **full 35-minute concerto** — the hardest
possible delivery, realistic only for someone who already holds all three movements, and the path
most likely to miss the timeline and trigger refunds against the guarantee. **Niching to recital
keeps your current offer but keeps its hardest, riskiest promise. Niching to auditions lets you
shrink the promise to something you can guarantee.**

## Why provability matters just as much

Step 2 rated *perceived likelihood of achievement* as the offer's thin driver. The audition/exam
niche fixes it structurally: outcomes are **binary and externally verified** — "3 students into
[conservatoire] this year," "passed LRSM with distinction." Those are the testimonials and the
guarantee teeth that recital outcomes ("she felt ready") can never match. **Choosing this niche
is the cheapest way to buy credibility.**

## Where recital genuinely wins (don't discard it)

- **Bigger, growing, year-round pool** — adult returners are the fastest-growing segment (8.9% CAGR)
  with disposable income and the strongest emotional pull toward "the whole concerto."
- **Less competition** — "recital prep coaching" is an unclaimed positioning.
- **Smoother demand** — no Dec–Feb cliff.
- **Higher LTV potential** — a recital relationship can run piece after piece.

This is why recital is the right **secondary / expansion** play: a higher-tier "Full Concerto"
product (priced up) once the audition engine is proven and you want to flatten seasonality.

---

## The sweet-spot sub-niche (the synthesis)

There's a hyper-niche that captures audition's strengths *and* recital's demographic:
**the adult diploma candidate (ABRSM ARSM / LRSM).**

- Deadline + **verifiable outcome** (pass/distinction) → audition's provability & urgency.
- **Adult, disposable income, year-round** exam sessions → recital's demographic & smoother demand.
- Programme-based → the **first-movement** product fits → honest short timeline.

If you want one phrase to build the whole offer around: *"Get your Tchaikovsky first movement to
distinction-standard for your diploma / audition — in [X] weeks, guaranteed."* It's the narrowest
point on the ladder where pain, money, provability, and deliverability all peak together.

---

## What this choice changes in the offer (if you commit to auditions/exams)

1. **Scope → first movement.** The 6–8 week timeline becomes honest; the full-concerto/12-week
   program becomes the *secondary* recital tier.
2. **Pre-screen recording bonus becomes core, not a bonus** — conservatoires *require* a pre-screen
   video; your "Stage-Ready Recording Setup" is now central to the outcome.
3. **Proof & guarantee rebuilt on verifiable results** (acceptances, passes, distinctions).
4. **Seasonal campaign calendar** — peak acquisition **Aug–Dec** ahead of Dec–Feb deadlines;
   use diploma candidates + recital tier to fill the off-season.
5. **Naming (MAGIC formula)** points at the avatar + dated goal: e.g.
   *"Audition-Ready Tchaikovsky — 8-Week First-Movement Intensive."*
6. **Hero copy fix** — your current hero already says "first movement," which *matches* the
   audition niche; it's the *full-concerto framing* elsewhere that would need to move to the
   secondary tier.

---

## Recommendation

**Niche down to auditions/exams**, with the **adult diploma candidate** as the sharpest entry
point and the **conservatoire/orchestral applicant** as the high-urgency seasonal spike. Treat
**recital (full concerto)** as a deliberately separate, higher-priced expansion tier — not the
spearhead. Then commit: stop straddling both, and rebuild the offer's scope, proof, and calendar
around the audition outcome. *(Updates [customer-avatar.md](customer-avatar.md), whose current
avatar assumes the full-concerto recital framing.)*
